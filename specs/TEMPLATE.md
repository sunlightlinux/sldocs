## The specification

Interfaces, formats, wire protocols, state transitions. Concrete enough
to implement against and to test against.

Where behaviour is inherited from something else — dinit, systemd,
elogind, s6 — say so and cite the source, because "compatible with X"
is a claim somebody will check.

## Deliberate divergences

Where this differs from the thing it is compatible with, and why. A
divergence without a recorded reason gets "fixed" by the next person.

rather than implementation — those cost the most to change later.
