# <requirement, stated as a capability>

**Status:** met | partially met | not met | abandoned

## What is required

One or two sentences, phrased so a reader can tell whether it holds.
"Boot reaches a login prompt in under two seconds on the reference
hardware" is checkable. "Boot should be fast" is not.

## Who needs it, and why

The requirement exists for someone. Say who, because that is what
decides the trade-offs when it conflicts with another requirement.

## How it is verified

The command, test or measurement that answers the question. If there
isn't one yet, say so — an unverifiable requirement is a wish, and
naming it as one is more useful than dressing it up.

## Related

Links to the spec that realises it, and to any finding that showed it
was not met.
